## 📌 What is uno.pro.bd Mail?

> [!NOTE]
> **uno.pro.bd Mail** is a **free custom-domain email service** — a Gmail-style webmail client that gives anyone a permanent, professional-looking email address (`yourname@uno.pro.bd`) without buying a domain or configuring a mail server.

Whether you need a clean address for a personal project, a portfolio, a resume, or just want something better than a random free-mail handle — this gives you a real inbox, on a real domain, for **zero cost**.

## 💡 Why It Exists

Getting a professional email address the traditional way means:

- ❌ Buying and renewing a domain
- ❌ Configuring DNS, MX records, SPF/DKIM
- ❌ Paying for or maintaining a mail server

**uno.pro.bd Mail removes all of that.** Sign in, pick a name, done.

```
1. Sign in with Google  →  2. Claim your username  →  3. Start sending & receiving mail
```
